VESPER privacy policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Who uses VESPER

VESPER is a personal assistant used only by its owner, Alec Bradley. It is not offered to the public.

What Google data it accesses

  • Gmail (read-only): recent messages are read to give the owner spoken and written summaries of his own email, for example in the morning briefing. VESPER cannot send, delete or change email.
  • Google Calendar: events are read to tell the owner about his day, and events are created or changed only when he asks.

How the data is handled

  • Data is processed on the owner's own computers. Email and calendar text may be sent to an AI model provider to write a summary; it is not used for advertising.
  • Google user data is never sold, shared with other people, or used for any purpose other than assisting the owner.
  • VESPER's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
